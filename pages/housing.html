What to Ask Before Signing

Never skip these questions when viewing an apartment.

📋

Contract & Legality

  • Is a written rental contract available?
  • Is the landlord the actual owner? Ask for property documents.
  • Is the contract in Azerbaijani + English/Russian?
  • Who handles utility bills — you or landlord?
🔑

Apartment Condition

  • Check hot water, heating, and electricity in all rooms.
  • Test internet speed / confirm provider available.
  • Check locks, windows, doors.
  • Ask about noise levels (above/below neighbours, street).
💰

Costs & Deposits

  • What is the security deposit? (Typically 1 month's rent.)
  • Are there agency fees? (Usually 50% of 1 month's rent.)
  • When and how is rent paid? Cash vs bank transfer?
  • What happens if you leave early?

Scam Avoidance Tips

⚠️ Never pay any money before you see the apartment in person and verify the landlord owns it.
  • ⛔
    Too cheap = red flagIf the price is significantly below market, be suspicious. Ask yourself why.
  • ⛔
    No contract offeredAlways insist on a written contract. Verbal agreements offer no protection.
  • ⛔
    Pressure to pay immediatelyLegitimate landlords allow you time to decide. Any urgency is a pressure tactic.
  • ✔
    Use recommended platformsTry tap.az or bina.az for listings. Ask fellow students for referrals too.
