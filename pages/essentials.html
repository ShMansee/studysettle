💱 Currency & Payments
CurrencyAzerbaijani Manat (AZN / ₼)
Exchange rate (approx.)1 USD ≈ 1.70 AZN  |  1 EUR ≈ 1.85 AZN
ATMsWidely available. Visa/Mastercard accepted at most.
Card paymentsCommon in malls, restaurants, supermarkets. Smaller shops prefer cash.
Best exchangeBank branches or official exchange booths (avoid street changers)
