📄 Documents

Step-by-step checklist for every document you need as an international student in Baku.

ℹ️ Always check official sources for the latest requirements. Regulations may change. This checklist is based on common student experiences.

✈️ Before Arrival

  • ✔
    Official Admission Letter Obtain your letter of acceptance from your university. Keep a printed and digital copy.
  • ✔
    Student Visa (if required) Apply at the nearest Azerbaijani embassy/consulate. Bring admission letter, passport photos, fee, and application form. MFA Azerbaijan — Consular Services ↗
  • ✔
    Passport Valid for at least 6 months beyond your planned stay. Make 3–4 photocopies before departure.
  • ✔
    Medical Certificate Some universities require a recent medical certificate (HIV test + general health). Check your university's requirements.
  • ✔
    Certified Diploma / Transcript Notarised and apostilled copies of your previous diplomas or high school certificate.

🏙️ On Arrival (First 30 Days)

  • ✔
    Temporary Residence Permit (Müvəqqəti Yaşayış İcazəsi) Apply at the State Migration Service within 15 days of arrival. Required documents: passport, invitation/admission letter, housing address, 2 photos, fee. State Migration Service ↗
  • ✔
    Registration of Address Register your residential address with local authorities. Your landlord or dormitory can often help with this.
  • ✔
    Student ID Card Collect from your university registrar after enrolment. Used for library access, discounts, and official identification.
  • ✔
    University Enrolment / Registration Complete official enrolment. Obtain enrolment certificate — needed for residence permit and other applications.

💳 Banking & SIM Card

  • ✔
    Open a Bank Account Popular options: ABB Bank, Kapital Bank, PASHA Bank. Bring passport + residence permit. Some banks allow account opening before residence permit.
  • ✔
    SIM Card Buy at operator shops (Azercell, Bakcell, Nar Mobile). Bring your passport. Starting plans from ~3–5 AZN. Data packages available.
  • ✔
    BakıKart (Transport Card) Rechargeable card for metro and buses. Get it at metro ticket offices for 2 AZN. Single ride: 0.40 AZN.

🏥 Health Insurance

  • ✔
    Health Insurance Policy Required for residence permit application. Check if your university provides group insurance, or purchase privately (PASHA Insurance, AXA, Ateshgah).
  • ✔
    Know Your Nearest Clinic Register with a local polyclinic. International students can use Baku clinics; carry your insurance card and passport at all times.
⚠️ Keep all original documents and photocopies in a safe place. Carry copies, not originals, for daily errands.
