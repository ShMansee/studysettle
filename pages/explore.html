🗺️ Explore Baku

Metro, buses, SIM cards, banks — everything to help you navigate the city from day one.

🚇 Getting Around

Baku has a clean metro, buses, and affordable taxis.

🚇

Baku Metro

The metro is the fastest way to cross the city. Three lines cover most central areas.

  • Single ride: 0.40 AZN
  • Get a BakıKart for ~2 AZN (reusable)
  • Operating hours: ~06:00 – 24:00
  • Announcements in Azerbaijani + English
🚌

Buses & Minibuses

A large network of city buses covers areas metro doesn't reach. Minibuses (marşrut) are older and cheaper but more crowded.

  • City bus: 0.30 AZN (BakıKart)
  • Minibus: 0.30–0.50 AZN (cash)
  • App: IABS for route planning
🚕

Taxis & Ride-Share

Safe, cheap, and available 24/7. Use apps to avoid negotiating fares.

  • Bolt — most popular, English UI
  • Uber — available in Baku
  • Taxi 189 — traditional taxi service
  • Short trip: ~2–5 AZN
🚲

Cycling & Walking

The Baku Boulevard (Bulvar) is great for walking along the Caspian. Cycling infrastructure is limited but growing.

  • Bike rental available at the Boulevard
  • City centre is walkable on flat terrain

📱 SIM Cards & Mobile Data

Get connected on arrival — bring your passport to any operator shop.

📶

Azercell

Largest network, best coverage across Azerbaijan including rural areas.

  • Starter pack: ~3–5 AZN
  • Monthly data bundles: 10–25 AZN
  • Available at all major malls and Azercell shops
Widest coverage
📶

Bakcell

Good urban coverage. Competitive data packages, popular among young users.

  • Starter pack: ~3 AZN
  • Unlimited social media add-ons available
Good value
📶

Nar Mobile

Budget-friendly option. Reasonable Baku coverage but weaker outside the capital.

  • Cheapest data packages
  • Good for Baku-only use
Budget pick

🏦 Banks

Open a local account to avoid currency exchange fees and make daily payments easier.

🏦

ABB Bank

State-backed, widespread ATM network. Popular for student accounts. Online banking available.

  • Bring passport + residence permit
  • Free basic account + debit card
🏦

Kapital Bank

One of the largest retail banks. Good mobile app (Birbank). Many ATMs and branches.

  • Multi-currency accounts
  • Mobile payments widely accepted
🏦

PASHA Bank

Premium service, strong for international transfers. Good option if you receive remittances.

  • SWIFT transfers
  • English-speaking staff in main branches

📸 Places to Visit

Explore the city when you're not studying.

🏛️

İçərişəhər (Old City)

UNESCO-listed walled old city with Maiden Tower, Palace of the Shirvanshahs, and dozens of restaurants and cafes. Free to walk through.

🌊

Caspian Boulevard

3 km waterfront promenade perfect for walks, cycling, and eating. Free entry, open 24/7. Hosts festivals and events year-round.

🛍️

Nizami Street

Main pedestrian shopping street with cafes, bookshops, clothing stores, and fast food. Good for casual hangouts.

🔥

Yanar Dağ (Fire Mountain)

Natural gas fire burning on a hillside — unique to Azerbaijan. A 30-min drive from the city. Entry ~2 AZN.
